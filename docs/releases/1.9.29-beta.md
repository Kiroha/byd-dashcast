# DashCast 1.9.29-beta

Satellite video now samples decoded HTML video counters independently of WebRTC statistics. A stuck or failed `getStats()` operation no longer suppresses that fallback or stops a decoder whose counters keep progressing. There is at most one unresolved stats operation per peer.

The first image has a fifteen-second budget after the answer is queued and a thirty-second overall startup cap. The existing six-second stale-image limit applies after decoding begins. Old cumulative counters, reset/replaced counter sources, packet traffic and control heartbeats cannot establish a fresh image. Replacement offers preempt pending negotiations without letting an older callback discard the new peer.

Bug reports retain bounded, typed receiver observations for the last eight video attempts: nullable RTP byte/packet/frame counters, decoded/key/dropped frames, DOM counters, codec and stats state/ages. These survive CarPlay/preview switching as historical evidence. They contain no SDP, routes, notifications, tokens or peer/network identities. Missing values remain unknown. Decode counters do not prove physical output, hardware decoding or reduced CPU/GPU use.

Keep DashSat **0.9.2-dev** installed; this correction needs no companion update or new pairing. While parked, retry sharing and leave **Preview received map** open for at least fifteen seconds. If it remains blank, send DashSat diagnostics to DashCast, wait for its confirmation and submit the normal DashCast bug report.

Validation: **1202 JVM tests across 205 suites** and **33 JavaScript tests** passed; debug assembly and debug/release lint passed. Lint retains two existing `UsableSpace` warnings per variant, with no errors. Release versionCode **668**. The software watchdog defect is reproduced and corrected; whether it caused the vehicle's black screen and actual video/performance behavior on the Tbox/DiLink remain unverified.
