# DashCast 1.8.32-beta

Three things that break while you are driving, fixed — and none of the three has been confirmed on
a real car yet. That is what this release is for.

> This is a tester pre-release from `switch-kotlin`. It is not merged into `main` and must not be marked as the repository's Latest release — `1.7.0` remains the stable one.

## Please read this part first

Everything below was found by reading the code, reasoned through, and fixed. None of it has been
seen working in a car. All three live in places nobody can reach remotely: the cluster screen, the
windshield arrow, the keyboard. These paths only exist when the car is switched on.

So this release is not "here are three fixes". It is **"here are three fixes, please tell us whether
they are real"**. If one misbehaves, it will misbehave while you drive — which is why the checks at
the bottom matter more than usual, and why keeping 1.8.31 within reach is sensible.

## Touch on the cluster comes back by itself

**Before.** If the helper process behind the mirror died — which it does occasionally, on its own —
the cluster stopped responding to your finger and stayed that way until you force-stopped DashCast.
The app went on talking to a process that no longer existed, and the machinery meant to repair that
was busy reconnecting a *different* helper that had never stopped working.

**Now.** It notices on the first touch that fails, lets go of the dead connection, and looks for the
new one at most once a second. In between, touches fall back to the slower internal route rather
than going nowhere. You may feel one dropped tap at the moment it happens. You should not have to
restart anything.

## The arrow on the windshield stops blinking out

**Before.** On a motorway with the next turn 40 km away, the distance on screen does not change for
roughly half a minute. DashCast concluded after 12 seconds that guidance had frozen and cleared the
arrow. The distance ticked, the arrow came back, and on a long approach this repeated the whole way.

**Now.** When your navigation app repeats a screen DashCast has already put on the HUD, that counts
as guidance still being alive, and the arrow stays.

**The honest version.** This trade has a cost. That 12-second timeout is what clears an arrow which
has *genuinely* gone stale — the reason it exists is a real incident where a dead nav app left a
frozen arrow on the windshield. It is now weaker. The guard is that only a repeat of a frame which
actually reached the HUD counts, so a nav app that stops posting is still caught. If you ever see an
arrow that stays up after guidance has really ended, that is the thing to report.

## The keyboard bridge sends what you actually typed

**Before.** You typed a destination, pressed Done, and the cluster received an empty field. Your
text was wiped microseconds before it was sent. The one job the bridge has, it did not do.

Two separate causes, both fixed. The text is now captured at the instant you press Done rather than
re-read a moment later; and the housekeeping that empties the box afterwards is no longer forwarded
to the cluster as if you had erased it yourself.

**And one you would never have guessed.** A destination typed but abandoned — you changed your mind
and dismissed the keyboard without validating — survived until the next time the bridge opened.
Pressing Done then, on an empty box, replayed the old address onto the cluster and routed the car to
it. No keystrokes needed. That draft is now forgotten when the keyboard closes.

## What we would like you to check

1. **Cluster touch.** With the mirror running and touch working, wait for the helper to die on its
   own, or kill it, then keep tapping. Touch should come back within a second or so without
   restarting DashCast.
2. **The arrow.** Guidance on a motorway with a manoeuvre several kilometres out. Watch through at
   least 30 seconds of unchanged distance. It must not blink. And tell us if it ever stays up after
   guidance has ended.
3. **The keyboard.** Type a destination, press Done. The cluster must receive it, search it, and
   still hold it afterwards. Then: type something, dismiss without pressing Done, reopen the
   keyboard and press Done on the empty box — nothing should be sent.

## Verification

375 unit tests. `assembleDebug`, `assembleRelease` with R8 and lint all green, zero errors and zero
missing translations. Release APK scanned: no credential pattern in any dex or resource.

A pre-release audit of these commits caught two things before you saw them: a blocking call this
release had put inside the screen-layout path, which would have frozen the interface when the mirror
helper was wedged rather than cleanly dead; and the abandoned-destination replay described above.

## If something goes wrong

Send a bug report from the app. Say which of the three you were using and what the car was doing.
Reinstalling 1.8.31-beta is safe if a fix makes things worse.
