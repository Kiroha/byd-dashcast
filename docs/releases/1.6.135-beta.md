# DashCast 1.6.135-beta

Race-safe background optimization after 1.6.134-beta, based on a second complete audit of production startup, lifecycle, mirror, screenshot, Hotspot, voice, shell, storage, and callback paths.

> This is a tester pre-release from `switch-kotlin`. It is not merged into `main` and is not the repository's Latest release.

## Highlights

- Removes the synchronous SurfaceFlinger dump from mirror-start latency without deleting the M7 diagnostic evidence.
- Orders screenshot capture, pull, prune, and clear operations so privacy deletion and report attachments cannot race JPEG writes.
- Suppresses redundant screenshot-prune shells only after a confirmed successful daemon prune, preserving the failure fallback.
- Halves visible Hotspot stats shell dispatches and avoids recreating unchanged client rows.
- Prevents paused Hotspot callbacks from updating the Activity or relaunching TetherFi in the background.
- Enforces one physical Hotspot probe at a time across pause/resume, preventing stale shell queue buildup.
- Removes repeated LocalBroadcastManager lookup and hidden-tab clock work from VoiceService.
- Adds 11 focused policy, payload, generation, and asynchronous-operation tests.

## Full changelog since 1.6.134-beta

### Mirror startup latency with diagnostic preservation

- `MirrorDaemon.setupMirror()` previously applied the complete SurfaceControl transaction and then synchronously ran `dumpsys SurfaceFlinger | grep ...` before returning success.
- The dump was diagnostic-only: its output never affected display-token ownership, layer stack, target surface, projection geometry, fallback selection, return value, or teardown.
- The same post-setup audit now runs after `Transaction.apply()` on a dedicated bounded serial executor.
- Mirror start returns immediately after its functional work and no longer waits for a full SurfaceFlinger dump/process exit.
- The existing M7 contract remains intact: `mirrordaemon_latest.log` still receives the post-setup token/layerStack evidence.
- The diagnostic worker permits one running audit and one queued audit. Additional rapid restarts log a skip instead of retaining an unbounded stale queue.
- All existing setup traces remain: begin dimensions, surface validity, display-token creation, layer-stack assignment, surface assignment, projection assignment, transaction application, failure details, and final destination geometry.

### Screenshot pruning with failure-safe ownership

- Every successful `TRANSACT_CAPTURE_DISPLAY` JPEG already invokes the uid-2000 daemon's in-process `pruneShotDir()` before returning.
- The app nevertheless ran a second shell-based prune every 30 seconds during active projection.
- `ClusterShotSchedulePolicy` now suppresses the app prune only when a successful daemon JPEG/prune was observed recently.
- Capture replies are classified from the existing `OK <path> <bytes>B` contract; failed, missing, or exceptional replies do not advance the daemon-prune timestamp.
- If the capture Binder is missing or a capture fails for 30 seconds, the historical app-side prune automatically resumes even while projection remains active.
- After projection stops, the periodic age/per-tag prune remains unchanged so post-incident screenshots survive briefly and then self-clean.
- Capture cadence remains 15 seconds; app fallback prune cadence remains 30 seconds; hard limits remain 12 total JPEGs and 5 minutes.

### Screenshot privacy and attachment race fixes

- Capture rounds, app pruning, screenshot pulling, and clearing now share the recorder's single ordered worker.
- Confirmed pre-fix race 1: turning the screenshot setting OFF could dispatch `rm` on the ADB executor while a JPEG was still being written on the recorder executor. The late capture could recreate a file after the privacy switch was disabled.
- Fix: `clear()` is now queued after all earlier capture work and executes its shell command synchronously only on the recorder worker.
- Confirmed pre-fix race 2: Bug Wizard bundling could list and pull a final-path JPEG while the daemon was still writing it, producing a partial/corrupt attachment.
- Fix: blocking `pullShotsInto()` submits its list/pull operation to the same recorder worker and waits from the existing Bug Wizard background thread.
- The only `pullShotsInto()` caller was verified to be the non-UI bundling thread; no main-thread wait was introduced.
- Bug-report consent, report-only fallback, zip naming, Telegram delivery, post-send deletion, and device-local privacy behavior remain unchanged.

### Visible Hotspot snapshot consolidation

- A visible `HotspotActivity` previously submitted two shell operations every 5 seconds:
  - one `dumpsys activity services` probe for TetherFi's `ProxyForegroundService`;
  - one combined Wi-Fi P2P, ARP, and neighbor inventory for connected clients.
- These reads now use one sentinel-delimited shell snapshot.
- `HotspotStatsPayload` validates the UP/DOWN service marker and passes the remaining payload unchanged into the existing client parser.
- A malformed or transiently failed snapshot preserves the last UI state rather than inventing a DOWN result or clearing clients.
- The independent 20-second Activity watchdog and process-wide `HotspotKeeper` keep their existing service-specific probe, restart ceiling, cooldown, overlay-permission guard, and recovery semantics.

### Hotspot render deduplication

- `HClient` is now a value data class, allowing complete client-list equality checks.
- Identical 5-second snapshots no longer call `removeAllViews()` and recreate every icon, name, and IP row.
- Changes to MAC, name, IP, order, or count still rebuild the visible list immediately.
- Empty-state visibility and client count behavior are unchanged.

### Hotspot lifecycle callback fix

- The local watchdog was removed from the Handler in `onPause()`, but an already-running shell callback could still return afterward.
- Because the settings switch remained checked, that stale callback could enter `handleWatchdogResult()` and relaunch TetherFi while the Activity was backgrounded, contradicting the local-watchdog lifecycle contract.
- `GenerationGate` now gives stats and watchdog callbacks restartable lifecycle generations.
- `onPause()`, local watchdog stop, and subsequent restart invalidate all earlier UI/restart tokens.
- Every callback checks its generation on the shell worker and again on the main thread before touching views or invoking restart logic.
- Returning to the Activity creates a fresh valid generation without permanently disabling future probes.

### Hotspot physical single-flight

- Lifecycle invalidation alone is not enough: setting a boolean false on pause could allow a new probe after resume while the previous command still physically occupied `ShellGateway`.
- `AsyncOperationGate` tracks the exact active callback-based operation independently from Activity lifecycle.
- Stats and watchdog probes cannot overlap, including across pause/resume or disable/re-enable transitions.
- A stale completion cannot release a newer operation because only its exact token can clear the gate.
- `ShellGateway` still delivers error callbacks for queue rejection and command failures, so gates are released on every normal completion path.
- If a shell command itself remains blocked, stats wait instead of adding replacement snapshots to the bounded global shell queue.

### VoiceService steady-state cleanup

- `VoiceService` now resolves and caches the process-local `LocalBroadcastManager` once in `Service.onCreate()`.
- State and 20 Hz level broadcasts reuse that cached singleton.
- When no Voice diagnostic subscriber exists, the capture loop no longer reads `SystemClock.elapsedRealtime()` for every 50 ms microphone frame.
- AudioRecord reads, pre-roll, wake-word/Vosk sample delivery, telemetry payloads, subscriber cadence, state/error broadcasts, and foreground-service behavior are unchanged.

## Deliberately unchanged candidates

- App-icon memory shedding was not added: invalidating only `AppRepository` would not free icons still retained by the adapter, favorites, holders, and ImageViews; a complete purge needs measured RSS evidence and atomic UI reload design.
- `ClusterService` move/diagnostic executors remain process-scoped to preserve ordering across `START_STICKY` service recreation and late teardown callbacks.
- The daemon's one-second trigger-file heartbeat remains unchanged because it is the proven fallback for lost `FileObserver` events on DiLink 5.
- TetherFi service health is not replaced by a PID/package check; only the existing service-specific probe can distinguish a running package from a working proxy service.
- ImageReader capture timing, projection cadence, R8/minification, layout flattening, Vosk pooling, and OEM reflection surfaces remain unchanged because they require profiling or on-car validation and have materially larger blast radius.

## Suggested tester checks

- Start the cluster mirror and toggle fullscreen several times; verify video and touch start immediately and M7 still shows the asynchronous SurfaceFlinger audit in the daemon log.
- Leave projection active for several screenshot rounds, then file a Bug Report with screenshots and verify every JPEG opens correctly.
- Disable screenshot capture during an active projection, wait beyond one capture interval, and verify no new device-side shots appear.
- Temporarily make the capture Binder unavailable and verify app-side age pruning still runs instead of leaving stale files indefinitely.
- Open Hotspot with TetherFi running and verify status, uptime, traffic, and connected clients update normally.
- Background Hotspot while a probe is running; verify the local Activity watchdog does not relaunch TetherFi or update the stopped screen.
