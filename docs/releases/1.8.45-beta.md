# DashCast 1.8.45-beta

Supersedes 1.8.44-beta. **1.8.44 told you your Wi-Fi hardware addresses were being removed. They
were not.** Correcting that is the whole reason this release exists.

> This is a tester pre-release from `audit/integr-1828`. It is not merged into `main` and must not be marked as the repository's Latest release — `1.7.0` remains the stable one.

## The correction

Yesterday's releases said that on the scan lines which leaked Wi-Fi network names, "the hardware
address sitting right next to the name was correctly hidden". That was false.

Your car masks part of those addresses itself, and writes them as `BSSID 14:0c:76:**:**:3f`. The
rule that removes addresses expected six pairs of hex digits and could not match that shape at all.
In the report I have been working from, **150 addresses covering 53 distinct access points** left
the car, each with its signal strength and the set of networks it was seen alongside. That
combination is enough to locate where a car was parked, and it concerns neighbours who never
consented to anything.

The fourteen addresses the report credited itself with removing were a different field on the same
lines, which happened to be fully hexadecimal.

**What this means for reports you have already sent.** Add this to the list in the 1.8.44 notes:
alongside the network names, and the position data that leaked before 22 August, the access-point
addresses around you have been going out in every report until this build. Nothing removed them.

Fixed, and verified by running the real report through the shipped code: 150 removed, none of any
form left behind.

## Three more of the same kind

All four found by auditing my own commits after publishing them — not before.

**The redaction footer was being destroyed by our own second pass.** Every report with screenshots
is redacted a second time on the way into the bundle, and the footer's own `wifi-ssid=59` looked
like a field to the network-name rule, so it came back as `wifi-ssid=<ssid:…>`. That is the exact
signal the 1.8.43 work added — a rule reporting `0` or `ERR` — turned back into noise, on the path
most reports take, because screenshots are on by default.

**The screen-layer section still could not reach the cluster.** 1.8.44 moved it past the preamble,
which was right and not enough: this car prints about ten lines per layer, so the window covered
eighteen of eighty-six, and those eighteen are the bottom of the stack. The cluster is drawn last.
It now keeps one line per layer and reaches all of them.

**The filtered log section came back empty and always would have.** It asked for the last 1500 lines
and then filtered them, which on a car logging four hundred lines a second is the final four
seconds. That is the section that would have carried the evidence for the Android Auto fix.

## Everything else is 1.8.44

The Android Auto fix, the nine report blind spots, the report pruning on Android 13 cars — all
unchanged, and the 1.8.44 notes describe them accurately. **Read them**, particularly the part about
what your older bug reports contained; this release adds one more item to that list rather than
replacing it.

## Why three releases in a day

Every one of these four defects has the same shape as the two that forced the last correction: a
check written against text I chose rather than text the car emits. The audit that found them was run
on my own commits after they shipped. It should have run before, and from now on it will.
