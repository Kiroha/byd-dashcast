# DashCast 1.9.51-beta

Fix **Hide the entire pane** after **Restore with the full sequence** when projection
masking is enabled. The button now reuses the complete Electro masking sequence after
fresh projection, power and firmware checks, with recovery saved before any write.
The independent one-frame fallback keeps its original SDK-value gate. Full restoration
continues to suspend automatic masking for the same projection owner until an explicit
Hide or a genuinely new projection session.

Fix recovery when stopping Classic or Fission projection, including service teardown and
close errors. DashCast cancels hiding immediately, waits for native projection closure
and at least one second of quiet, then requests the full restoration sequence: 40 restore
frames, command 12, a 1,200-ms wait and command 53. Known masking debt is retained across
an owner replacement so a later Stop can recover it; saved-value one-frame restoration
remains an independent fallback while full recovery is pending. Delivery still does not
confirm the visible state.

The maintainer confirmed initial projection masking and full restoration with
**1.9.50-beta on the car**. **The new re-hide and Stop cycles still require parked-vehicle
validation.** Command 53 still forces ancillary ADAS type 0 rather than restoring a
captured prior type; implementation details remain in `docs/NATIVE_ADAS_PANE.md`.

**Settings → ADAS pane: hide / restore → Hide the ADAS pane during projection** remains
off by default. **Hiding the entire pane also hides its distance alerts and lane warnings.**
The existing restriction to the researched **BYD SEAL, Android 10 / DiLink 3, SX361** firmware
`6125f_1for2_USER_SIGN_SX361_202606100404_Q2700` and its exact captured Android fingerprint
is unchanged. Other fingerprints are refused.

**Install the APK manually over the existing DashCast application without uninstalling.**
The application displays **1.9.51-beta · build 690**. **DashSat 0.9.9-dev remains compatible
and does not need an update.** No satellite protocol or guidance listener changes are included.

**1.9.0 remains stable.**
