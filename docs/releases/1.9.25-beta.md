# DashCast 1.9.25-beta

DashSat can now send its recent video diagnostics directly to DashCast over the existing authenticated local connection. This avoids needing Telegram or WhatsApp internet access on the external box.

In DashSat 0.9.2-dev, choose **Send diagnostics to DashCast**, wait for received confirmation, then return to DashCast and send a normal bug report. The receiver works in the background while CarPlay is visible. A video viewer is not required.

The report includes one strictly typed, bounded, sender-reported historical snapshot with fixed stages/states/causes, counters, local attempt ordinals and elapsed ages. No SDP, credentials, addresses, peer IDs, route text or notification content is transferred. The RAM cache keeps it for up to 24 hours after receipt, including ordinary disconnects, and clears on disable, revoke, restart or different/unknown-peer authentication.

This release preserves the targeted firmware SYSTEM export from 1.9.24-beta. The diagnostic transfer does not itself fix or establish successful vehicle video; real Tbox video/performance acceptance remains pending.
