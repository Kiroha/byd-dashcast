# DashCast 1.9.40-beta (679)

DIAG groups targeted captures under **“Relevés locaux OEM”** (“Local OEM captures”). **“Métadonnées QR — 16 lectures”** (“QR metadata — 16 reads”) keeps the previous capture; **“Identité du service OEM — 23 lectures”** (“OEM service identity — 23 reads”) adds metadata needed to identify the missing native implementation of an OEM service.

The new closed profile `OEM_STRATEGY_SERVICE_METADATA_V1` reads daemon identity and Android user, the clock and six specific system properties, the declared presence of `strategyservice`, the service list, and the numeric UID/PID/PPID columns and process names. It requests metadata and the literal link for five candidate paths `/{system,vendor,odm,product,system_ext}/bin/strategyservice`; it does not follow returned targets. If the observed identity is not unambiguously shell UID 2000, subsequent reads are not attempted.

A fixed reader filters `/init*.rc` files and `.rc` files in the five directories `/{system,vendor,odm,product,system_ext}/etc/init` and their single `hw` subdirectory. It exports service names, executable paths **without arguments**, associated `user`, `group`, and `seclabel` attributes, and literal import paths. It follows no imports or links, exports no complete file contents, comments, or other directives, and executes no collected text. Limits are 512 files, 64 KiB per file, 8 MiB of total source, 512 lines per file, 1024 bytes per line, and 192 KiB of filtered output. A denial, unresolved value, continuation, or limit leaves the status incomplete; absence within this scope does not establish global absence.

These commands only read system sources. DashCast writes the local ZIP and its metadata; the existing transport may create its temporary PID file. No OEM tool is opened, no OEM policy or authentication method is called, no QR code or code is requested or validated, and no setting, permission, country, DNS, or certificate is changed. This capture demonstrates neither access to the tools, independent approval, nor a QR bypass.

The already-connected PROXY transport, time limits, and diagnostic queuing while a Binder call remains active are preserved. Progress distinguishes recognized reads from incomplete results. The ZIP `oem-service-001.zip` and result/integrity files remain under `Android/data/com.byd.dashcast/files/oem-service-exports/` in the user's primary internal storage. Share manually using **“Fichiers du relevé → Lister / partager”** (“Files from capture → List / share”); there is no automatic transfer. **“Relevés précédents”** (“Previous captures”) lets you find either profile without starting another capture; the list examines at most 128 entries per profile and displays at most 20 captures. Keep the vehicle parked and DIAG visible during capture.

The 14-read context plan, 16-read QR profile, and full export retain their existing scope and archive identity. Local checks cover closed commands and quoting, the reader executed on synthetic files, denials and limits, shell identity, archives and their hashes, retrieval, and profile selection. Compatibility of the new capture with the vehicle ROM still needs on-vehicle validation.

A **partial** result is expected if candidate paths are absent, `readlink` targets an ordinary file, a property is empty, or some init directories are unavailable. The ZIP remains useful: share it without repeating the capture solely because of this status. Reads and path checks are not atomic and do not constitute a hardware attestation.
