# DashCast 1.9.42-beta

The OEM service identity collector now reads init metadata in 32 independent pages instead of
one long shell command. A returned DiLink 3 report showed the original reader hitting the
30-second command deadline, which discarded its accumulated output. An offline fixture
reproduced this failure with modest injected utility latency; the exact vehicle-side cause
remains unconfirmed.

DIAG → Relevés locaux OEM → Identité du service OEM now offers 54 fixed reads. Each init page
selects at most 16 ordinal entries before running file utilities, with its own 256 KiB source,
6 KiB filtered output and 12-second soft time budget. The 32 page budgets total 512 selected
slots, 8 MiB of source reads and 192 KiB of filtered output. Page identity, completion, limits
and omissions are recorded separately. Earlier replies remain in the local ZIP if a later
page reaches the normal command deadline; a fatal transport failure still stops collection
without reconnecting or retrying.

Several blocked pages can take substantially longer than the usual few minutes: the unchanged
30-second command deadlines alone can total 16 minutes across 32 pages, plus collection and
archive overhead. Cancellation keeps returned replies after the current call returns; a stuck
physical Binder call can remain quarantined rather than starting overlapping work.

The screen explains page progress, cancellation and expected duration. Previous V1 archives
remain accessible and shareable with their original profile rather than being described as
new V2 collections. The existing 14-read context and 16-read QR profiles retain their commands.

Source reads remain restricted to the same literal init roots and metadata fields. Imports
and links are not followed, process arguments and raw init source are not exported, and no
OEM policy or authentication method is called. Pages are independent, non-atomic observations:
entry changes or page limits can cause omissions or overlap. This build does not demonstrate
a QR bypass, identify an unavailable server implementation or establish global absence.

Validation: 54 targeted reader, export, UI, legacy-profile and transport tests pass, including
all 32 ordinal pages over a 513-entry fixture, actual source/output ceilings, clock anomalies,
post-read deadlines, page-timeout isolation, cancellation and original V1 archive recovery.
Actual DiLink 3 page timing and metadata availability remain unverified until the targeted
vehicle report is returned. Final signing and artifact checks accompany the frozen release.
