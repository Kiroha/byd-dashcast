# DashCast 1.9.35-beta

Fixes the full-export inventory entering SD/USB contents. **DIAG → Export complet accessible** now
collects only readable ordinary files on attested internal storage: firmware, internal data and the
internal emulated storage view, through ProxyDaemon (SHELL UID 2000) exclusively.

Filesystem type and familiar paths do not prove physical provenance. The collector records bounded,
read-only mountinfo, StorageManager (`sm list-volumes all` / `sm get-primary-storage-uuid`) and fixed
kernel block attributes for observed devices, their partition parents, dm slaves and loop backings.
USB ancestry, SD type, removable devices, adopted volumes and their bind/FUSE/backing aliases are
excluded before directory snapshots or payload reads. Ambiguous or unavailable provenance is excluded
with an explicit reason. Internal MMC/UFS/boot-controller dependencies are checked; `removable=0`
alone is insufficient. Ordinary rootfs/tmpfs files remain eligible outside the existing pseudo/device
and DashCast-output exclusions. No arbitrary recursive sysfs scan is introduced.

The new profile/schema refuses older inventories with a different collection scope before contacting the
collector transport. Existing archives are preserved; choose **Nouvelle collecte du stockage interne**.
The public app export destination under `/storage/emulated/<user>/Android/data/com.byd.dashcast/files`
and the snapshot filesystem must be attested internal before creating a session, and destination
provenance is rechecked before new ZIP/manifest files. No private or external fallback is used.
If an output attestation is lost, the session permanently stops writing and reports the failure in
DIAG; finished archives are retained. Temporary cleanup removes only known snapshot files, then
attempts to remove the empty directory without recursively entering any child mount.

Progress, deferred cancellation, bounded disk inventory, approximately 1 GiB ZIP volumes, storage
reserves and SHA-256 verification on resume are preserved. ProxyDaemon protocol 26 and its strict FD
read capability are preserved. Additive transaction 40 and its required capability provide closed,
bounded metadata operations without the legacy shell PID-file wrapper; bootstrap mountinfo retains
its exact line endings. Fixed-argv getters use bounded process-group lifecycle and no PID files. Collection does not execute extracted files, alter permissions or
OEM settings, remount storage or disable protections. Reads and destination checks are live and
non-atomic; desktop tests do not establish vehicle runtime compatibility or duration.

Use with the vehicle stationary and DIAG visible. Results may contain personal data; archives stay
local and sharing remains manual. A partial manifest records inaccessible files, excluded/unknown
supports and collection limits. Copy every final ZIP and the external manifest.json.
