# DashCast 1.9.18-beta — Compact cluster music comparison

This dedicated pre-release adds three parked diagnostic formats to compare whether
shorter musical text leaves more room for navigation on the OEM cluster. It also
corrects the OEM roundabout circulation mapping. **It does not automatically move
music to the right or resolve the shared text-area overlap.**

**Vehicle APK: versionName `1.9.18-beta`, versionCode `657`.** Install manually
over the existing DashCast app, **without uninstalling**, to preserve settings and
Satellite pairing. Publication continues from `feat/hud-2.0`; **1.9.0 remains the
stable release**.

## Compare music layouts on the vehicle

With the vehicle parked, temporarily disable automatic cluster music in Settings,
then open **Diag → HUD Bench (DL3) → Test music on cluster**. Keep the same active
guidance for each comparison and confirm the parked acknowledgement for every run.

- **Artist + title**: the reference format.
- **Short title**: `DashCast` without an artist.
- **Minimal text**: `DC` without an artist.

Each run lasts six seconds and attempts to clear its acquired test output. Compare the icon
and title position against the maneuver distance, and provide photos and a bug
report for the same vehicle/firmware. Restore your automatic music setting after
testing. All comparison labels and help are translated into the existing 13 languages.

Shorter text is an experimental positioning hypothesis: inspected OEM themes
center the musical icon/text group, but their applicability to this exact firmware
is not confirmed. The cluster can scroll or render differently. **Vehicle validation
remains pending; SDK acceptance alone does not prove visible positioning.**

## Music and roundabouts

Production music remains independent of guidance and **is not hidden during
cluster guidance**. No automatic text-shortening or music positioning command is
added. The existing overlap remains unresolved until the comparison is evaluated.
Media recovery, bounded retries and redacted bug-report diagnostics are retained.

Correct the OEM roundabout families: right-hand traffic (France) uses the
counter-clockwise family; left-hand traffic (United Kingdom) uses the clockwise
family. Apply the corresponding correction to direction supplied by guidance,
preserving exit numbers and ordinary turn arrows. Confirm the displayed direction
and exit on the vehicle; static tests do not establish OEM rendering.

## Satellite compatibility

Compatible with **[DashSat 0.6.0-dev](https://github.com/Kiroha/dashcast-satellite/releases/tag/v0.6.0-dev)**.
Six-digit pairing and guidance **protocol v1 remain unchanged**; this comparison
requires no new companion release. Existing Android 10 ART startup and TLS identity
migration fixes are retained. A receiver migrating from the old v1 identity needs
fresh pairing because its certificate changes; an already working v2 identity
continues unchanged. Local media concerns sessions on the vehicle tablet;
Carlinkit media transport remains unsupported.

## Validation and limits

Release checks cover the full JVM suite, release lint, debug and signed non-debuggable
R8 release assembly, WebView/relay/scanner tests and the trusted release-asset scanner.
The stock Android 29 regression uses the exact frozen minified APK and matching
mapping with real AndroidKeyStore, pinned TLS/WSS authentication and reconnection.
This emulator check does not prove vehicle hardware or cluster positioning.

**Actual vehicle validation remains to be done.** Record the model, firmware and
music/guidance source when comparing the formats. OEM radio/Bluetooth/CarPlay
priority and recovery after testing also require physical confirmation. No new
privilege, vehicle-control transport or bypass of OEM restrictions is introduced.
