# DashCast 1.9.22-beta — Satellite viewer ordering and video evidence

A concurrent viewer attachment could advertise video readiness before the control receiver queued
an older viewer-unavailable rejection. DashSat could then mistake that late rejection for a new
viewer closure and wait indefinitely. The receiver now queues rejection under the same monitor
used for viewer attachment, preserving rejection-before-ready order without waiting for network I/O.

**Vehicle APK: versionName `1.9.22-beta`, versionCode `661`.** Install over the existing DashCast
application without uninstalling. Pairing, certificate pins, settings, generic roundabout guidance,
the package and usual platform signing identity are preserved. Use **DashSat 0.9.1-dev** on the
Carlinkit/Tbox for the companion readiness protection and retained video history. No new pairing
is required. **1.9.0 remains the stable release.**

## Diagnose video after switching from CarPlay

The user-authorized sharing session can start in DashSat before a receiver viewer is visible.
Accept Android capture consent, leave CarPlay, then open **Preview received map** in DashCast.
Hiding that viewer still releases its peer and decoder. Returning to CarPlay therefore shows
**waiting for DashCast** on DashSat even when it attempted video while the viewer was visible.
The same valid continuous capture can resume a viewer; terminal capture/control loss still
requires new Android consent. No invisible receiver decoder or background OEM projection is added.

Receiver bug reports now include bounded **Satellite video** stages retained across display
switches: viewer readiness, received offer, queued answer, track/media connection, decoded-frame
progress and fixed failure categories. The newest event is first; at most 32 entries and
saturating scalar totals are retained in memory. Frequent ICE events update counters only.
Hidden or replaced WebView bridge callbacks cannot report progress for the current viewer.

The companion's **Recent video activity** and **Copy video diagnostics** complement these stages
with capture, hardware encoder, offer and answer evidence. Queued replies do not establish
transmission, encoded frames do not establish decoding, and previous decoded frames do not
establish the current visible image. No tokens, SDP, routes, notifications, network/device
identities, raw errors or arbitrary JavaScript console text are retained.

errors and warnings**, debug assembly and **18 receiver JavaScript tests**. Final optimized artifact
identity, signing and Android 10 runtime checks are recorded alongside the frozen build evidence.
Regression checks cover the
actual receiver hub monitor ordering, stale control generations, bounded/private histories,
obsolete WebView bridge callbacks and JavaScript decoded-frame evidence. The wire contract and
fixtures are unchanged; DashSat remains pinned to reviewed commit
`784ad22e2ffd40d354b16d0f195cd10794b47454`.

blank video. Actual Tbox Ultra 1 / Android 15 to DiLink 3 / Android 10 video, the vehicle's installed
WebView version, hardware decoding, latency and vehicle CPU/GPU savings remain to be validated.
